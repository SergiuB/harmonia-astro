I am Lasantha, an Ayurveda and Spa Therapist from Sri Lanka. I began my career as a therapist in 1996 and have since dedicated my professional life to bodywork and holistic treatments.

Over the years, I have had the opportunity to work in five-star hotels and resorts in Sri Lanka, including Anantara Resort & Spa and Ramada Resort & Spa. Working with international guests has given me extensive experience in providing treatments for people from different backgrounds and with different needs.

With almost 30 years of experience as a therapist, I continue to approach each treatment with care, presence and attention to the individual needs of every person.

My treatments include Deep Tissue Massage, Stress Release Massage, Signature Massage, Back Massage, Head, Neck & Shoulder Massage, Face Massage and Foot Massage.

My intention is to create a comfortable and relaxing space where you can slow down, reconnect with your body and receive a treatment adapted to your needs.
