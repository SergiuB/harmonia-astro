More than 12 years ago, I walked into a yoga studio in Cluj for the first time. Not because I was flexible or passionate about movement, but because my back hurt, my body felt stiff, and stress had left its mark on me.
 
I still remember that first class. Breathing seemed harder than any posture, my body trembled during the simplest movements, and the next day every muscle hurt. It wasn't easy. And yet, something made me come back. 
Little by little, the pain began to decrease. My body became stronger and more mobile, while my mind became calmer. 
 
That's when I understood that yoga doesn't only change the way you move, but also the way you live. 
The transformation I experienced inspired me to complete an accredited Hatha Yoga teacher training after moving to Bucharest. 
Since then, yoga has accompanied me every day and has become a part of who I am. 
 
Over time, I felt that I was no longer looking only for a strong and flexible body, but also for more stillness, presence, and inner balance. That's how I ended up in Greece, where I completed my Yin Yoga training — a practice that taught me the power of slowing down and listening to my own body. 
 
Today I teach people who see themselves in my story: those who experience back pain, feel tired and stressed, and live at a fast pace. 
 
I believe yoga is for every body, not just for those who are flexible. You don't have to be in a certain shape to begin. You only need to be willing to give yourself a little time and a little patience. 
 
In my classes, I want to guide you towards reconnecting with yourself, with more gentleness, presence, and authenticity. To leave with a more relaxed body, a calmer mind, and the feeling that you have done something good for yourself. 
 
If you see yourself in my story, perhaps your journey can also begin with a single class. 
 
🌿 Because yoga isn't about perfect poses. It's about feeling at home in your own body.
