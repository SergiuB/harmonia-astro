I am Oleg Galkin, an applied kinesiologist, manual therapist, massage therapist and certified instructor in the SJ-DTR method.

My professional practice focuses on the relationship between the nervous system, muscles and movement. I use an integrative approach combining principles of applied kinesiology, manual muscle testing, techniques inspired by the Su Jok system, reflex point stimulation and manual therapy.

The SJ-DTR method (Su Jok – Deep Tendon Reflex) focuses on assessing and stimulating neuromuscular responses through specific techniques. It involves working with sensory receptors and reflexes associated with body awareness, muscle tone and movement coordination.

During sessions, I aim to identify areas of tension, muscular responses and possible functional limitations, adapting the techniques to each individual's needs.

Alongside the SJ-DTR and Quantum Body Correction approaches, I offer reflexology, deep tissue massage and manual therapy, as well as combined treatments that allow for a more comprehensive approach to the body.

My goal is to support muscle relaxation, mobility and physical comfort through an individualised approach that takes each person's needs into account.

I visit Bucharest once a month, where I welcome clients at Harmonia Terra. The rest of the time, I practise in Constanța, Romania.
